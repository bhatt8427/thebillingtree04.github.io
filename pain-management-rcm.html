Where Administrative Work Gets Complex

Prior Authorization

Procedure and medication requirements can differ by payer, making tracking and follow-up especially important.

Procedure Documentation

Accurate documentation and charge capture help reduce avoidable billing rework after the visit.

A/R Follow-Up

Outstanding balances often require structured payer follow-up, denial review, and clear escalation back to the practice.

What We Pay Attention To

Authorization Gaps

Missing or expired authorizations can interrupt scheduling and create downstream claim issues.

Documentation Handoffs

Clinical notes, procedure details, and charge information need to move cleanly into billing.

Denial Patterns

Repeated payer issues are easier to address when denial reasons are categorized and reviewed consistently.
